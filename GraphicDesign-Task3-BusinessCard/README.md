# Task 3 – Business Card Design

## Project Overview

A business card design created for The Second Home Café, following the brand's established visual identity.

## Task

Design a professional two-sided business card that communicates essential contact information while maintaining consistency with the brand identity.

## Deliverables

- Front-side business card
- Back-side business card
- Final PDF

## Tools Used

- Adobe Illustrator

##Assets
Google

## Design Approach

The design uses the café's established colour palette, typography, logo system, and botanical visual elements to create a cohesive and professional brand touchpoint.

The Second Home Café
