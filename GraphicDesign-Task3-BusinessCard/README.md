# Task 3 – Business Card Design

## Project Overview

A professional two-sided business card designed for **The Second Home Café**, following the visual identity established during Task 2.

## Objective

The objective was to create a professional and functional business card that communicates essential contact information while maintaining consistency with the café's established brand identity.

## Design Approach

The business card uses the café's established colour palette, typography, logo system, and botanical visual elements to create a cohesive brand touchpoint.

The layout was designed with clear information hierarchy, balanced spacing, and deliberate use of whitespace to maintain readability across both sides of the card.

## Brand Consistency

The design follows the visual language developed for **The Second Home Café**, ensuring that the business card works as a consistent extension of the brand identity.

Key elements carried forward include:

- Logo
- Colour palette
- Typography
- Botanical visual elements
- Overall visual tone
