# OIBSIP — Graphic Design Internship

A collection of design projects completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Graphic Design Internship**.

This repository contains the final deliverables for the five assigned graphic design tasks, organized separately for easy review.

---

## 🎨 Internship Tasks

### Task 1 — Event Posters & Fliers
Promotional and event-based poster designs developed with a focus on visual hierarchy, typography, composition, and colour balance.

📁 `GraphicDesign-Task1-EventPosters`

---

### Task 2 — Logo Design
A minimalist visual identity developed for the fictional brand **The Second Home Cafe**, focusing on nature, coffee, belonging, and growth.

📁 `GraphicDesign-Task2-LogoDesign`

---

### Task 3 — Business Card
A professional double-sided business card designed using the visual identity developed for The Second Home Cafe.

📁 `GraphicDesign-Task3-BusinessCard`

---

### Task 4 — Infographic
An informational infographic designed to communicate a topic through structured content, visual elements, icons, and clear information hierarchy.

📁 `GraphicDesign-Task4-Infographic`

---

### Task 5 — Magazine / Brochure
A multi-page editorial design developed with a consistent visual system, typography, imagery, layout, and page structure.

📁 `GraphicDesign-Task5-Magazine`
