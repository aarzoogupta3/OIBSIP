# Task 2 – Logo Design

## Project Overview

A logo design project focused on developing a distinctive visual identity for the fictional café brand **The Second Home Café**.

## Objective

The objective was to create a memorable and versatile logo that communicates the brand's personality, values, and visual direction while maintaining a clean and approachable identity.

## Brand Concept

**The Second Home Café** is imagined as a calm, welcoming space built around coffee, nature, comfort, community, and a sense of belonging.

The identity was developed to communicate the idea of a place where people can slow down, feel comfortable, and connect with their surroundings.

## Design Approach

The logo combines a home-inspired form with botanical and coffee-related elements to represent warmth, nature, growth, comfort, and belonging.

The visual language uses an earthy colour palette and a simple badge-style composition to create a warm and recognizable identity suitable for a café environment.
